This album “Phase Out” remakes the methodology of Steve Reich’s early works, in particular “Come out” and “Piano Phase”. But whereas Reich used analog technology, a voice recording, and the hands of pianists, Rothkamm computes digitally with samples of organ tones and marimbas. The difference here is in precision and resolution. The various psychoacoustic effects of phase shifting can now be heard with absolute time precision and in microtonal resolution: 4 organ chords are played on 3 slightly detuned organs on the aptly titled “Four Chords on Three Organs,” and a random sequence of notes is played on 2 marimbas on “Random Sequence on Two Marimbas”. Sounds all the same or are there worlds of differences? You decide.
