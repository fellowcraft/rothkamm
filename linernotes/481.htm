Produced between 1993 and 1994 at Frank Genius studio in the Lower East Side of Manhattan and Williamsburg, Brooklyn. Remastered in 2017 at Lodge For Utopian Science in View Park, California. Re-sequenced in 2018 at UNI-Center Cologne, Germany and Flat 33 in London, UK. Special thanks to Peter Scherer and Nina Schneider. All female vocals by Barbara Gogan.
