Behold the Snail. It lives next to the Tentacles under the sea. Behold the Tentacles, they live next to the Snail. Their adventures are manifold and despite all the commotion not much moves forward. This is ok. The speed of movement is irrelevant compared to its magnitude. Very little can move you a lot, like the will that moves the mountain or a flag in the wind. You breathe in, you breathe out, this is what you always do. The Snail moves on and eventually, eventually it will get past the Tentacles while you hold your breath. None of this will last. You breathe, the Tentacles move and the Snail passes you by. The distance will grow on you while the Tentacles tangle and entangle. Not an allegory, this is the nature of reality itself.
