Life in Los Angeles is luxurious until it isn’t. It is all about driving. Always about getting somewhere. Most of an Angeleno’s time is spent on getting something out of life. Perhaps a reward or just a retribution, an equalization of forces. Pull, Push. When you are stuck in traffic, everything changes. This is what life is all about? So, the car failed the smoke test. It is not an old car, you see, no, it is only 8 years old. Come on. Why would it do that? Of course, we Californians take pride in our Platonic idea of an emissions-free society. Prestige air for everyone, everywhere. California can be such a place and only the car is holding us back. So I went to the Volkswagen downtown dealership. Well, it is all about the money. How much are you willing to spend? Anything to pass a smoke test? Yes! So I had the car put through tests it passed with flying colors, only to be left with good advice: Drive the car at least 50 miles (ca. 80 km) before you attempt another smoke test. So, this is indeed what I did. My relatives in Newbury Park were that far away in the endless expanses of Southern California. A city that never seems to end. So I took on this expedition of driving for the sake of driving. It was a smooth ride to Newbury Park.
