In 1996 Alfred 23 Harth produced the CD Pollock under the proviso that the composers he invited draw on material from his previously out-of-print LPs. Frank Rothkamm was one of them, as were Elliott Sharp, Peter Tomas Fey and Harth himself. Pollock was released in 1997. Previously, due to Harth's contact with the label 23 in Frankfurt/Main, a 12" vinyl A Two Three Age was released as an excerpt from Pollock, written by Alfred Harth and Frank Rothkamm, with a photography by Gerald Domenig.
