Produced in 1995 at Flux Records Studios in Williamsburg, Brooklyn. Mastered in 2017 at Lodge For Utopian Science in View Park, CA. Edited in 2018 at UNI-Center Studio in Cologne, Germany. Contains voice samples by: Patrick Stewart (from "The Simpsons"), Herbert Eimert, Carol King, Karlheinz Stockhausen, Padmini Rao, Bounkam Solo from Nonesuch's “Music of West Africa", Corin Curschellas (from each track of her "Rappa Nomad" album), Ringo Starr, John Lennon, George Harrison, Paul McCartney and Frank Genius (Inhaling). Special thanks to Nina Schneider, Peter Scherer and Corin Curschellas.
