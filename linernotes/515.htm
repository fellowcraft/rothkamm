You always say less is more but then end up filling up the space you encounter. Filling something minimally is hard as hell. Indeed, you treat absence as a form of presence. An empty space is a space filled with something you can’t imagine yet. But to fill it with as little as possible is difficult, because very little should still make sense. Nothing does not make any sense, because nothing that makes sense is not more or less than something. Well, you could signify nothing and say: The least amount of something stands in for nothing. Because no matter how hard you try, when you look at nothing, you’ll always find something. Even in empty space. All this is not a tragedy. You just look at the very minimal amount that could stand by itself, without standing in for something. It will signify nothing. This is just enough if you give it time. Now you neither express something nor nothing. Perhaps something in between, perhaps not. You are not on shaky ground here, you have something to hold on to, for a very long time. It isn’t bad, no, it is good enough to be something and signify nothing. Subtle, that is.
